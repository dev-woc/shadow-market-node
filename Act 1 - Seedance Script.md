# Act 1 - Seedance 2.0 Script

> Shot-by-shot Seedance 2.0 prompt script for Act 1 ("The Infiltration," Scenarios 1-5). Two deliverables: one cinematic trailer stitching the whole arc together, and five standalone clips that can stand alone as social teasers. See [[Act 1 - The Infiltration]] for the source story beats and [[Vulnerable Store Tech Spec]] for the visual style this is built from.

---

## Visual Style Primer (apply to every prompt below)

Translated from the tech spec's terminal/hacker aesthetic into Seedance-friendly prompt language — keep this consistent across all 12 shots so the trailer and the 5 standalone clips read as one world.

- **Color grade:** crushed near-black blacks (`#0a0a0a`), monochrome phosphor-green glow (`#00ff00`) as the dominant light source on faces/surfaces, alert-red (`#ff0040`) flash accents reserved for danger/exploit/audit beats only. Everything else desaturated toward grayscale.
- **Lighting:** single-source glow — screens and terminals are the only light in frame, lighting faces and rooms from below/in front. High contrast, deep shadow falloff.
- **Camera:** handheld micro-shake for realism on establishing shots; slow deliberate dolly-in on screens during tension beats; hard glitch-cut whip pans on exploit moments.
- **Texture/FX:** CRT scanlines and subtle chromatic aberration baked into every screen shot; analog glitch/tear artifacts triggered specifically on "exploit" actions (refund spam, bot swarm, palindrome check); faint ASCII-art texture in background set dressing.
- **Typography on screen:** monospace terminal font, matrix green on black, red for alerts/errors.
- **Audio bed (global):** low synth drone under everything, mechanical keyboard clicks as a recurring motif, rising tension pulse into every "reveal."

## Part 1: Act 1 Trailer (~45s, 7 shots)

### Shot 1 — Cold Open (5s)
**Prompt:** Extreme close-up on a young hacker's face lit only by a laptop screen in a pitch-black room, matrix-green glow flickering across their eyes. Their fingers type rapidly. Camera slowly pushes in. Handheld micro-shake.
**On-screen text:** URL bar typing out `shadow-market.onion` → screen flashes red → `ACCESS DENIED`
**Sound:** Low drone, keyboard clicks, a single sharp error buzz on the flash.

### Shot 2 — The Gatekeeper (6s)
**Prompt:** Screen-capture-style shot of a dark storefront UI, a grid of shady items with price tags, a cursor rapidly clicking two items into a cart. Cart total ticks upward. Glitch-cut to a glowing green "VERIFY" button.
**On-screen text:** `$100.00 EXACT` / `VERIFICATION REQUIRED`
**Sound:** Tense synth pulse, a soft price-blip per item selected, satisfying chime on total match.

### Shot 3 — The Infinite Money Glitch (6s)
**Prompt:** Close-up on a cursor rapid-clicking a glowing "REFUND" button in an admin console, credit counter spinning upward chaotically in the background, screen glitch-tearing with each click, faint red alert light starting to pulse in a corner.
**On-screen text:** counter spinning `0 → 1,000,000` / small flickering `AUDIT BOT: INCOMING`
**Sound:** Rapid click SFX, glitch stutter, rising alarm klaxon undertone building tension.

### Shot 4 — The Wash (6s)
**Prompt:** A glowing green candlestick price chart on screen, a cursor marking a dip then a peak, a sharp upward flash connecting the two points like a lightning bolt across the chart.
**On-screen text:** `BUY LOW` → `SELL HIGH` / profit counter ticking green
**Sound:** Market-ticker beeps accelerating, a satisfying upward whoosh on the sell.

### Shot 5 — The Bot Swarm (6s)
**Prompt:** A raw, unranked vote feed scrolling rapidly on screen — no leaderboard, just a flood of vote entries — the username "you" repeating over and over as the feed floods faster and faster, screen edges glitching under the volume.
**On-screen text:** `SELLER OF THE MONTH` / vote tally flicker crossing `>50%`
**Sound:** Rapid-fire notification spam building into a swarm-like buzz, cutting to a triumphant sting.

### Shot 6 — The Secret Knock (6s)
**Prompt:** A single blinking cursor in a stark terminal input field in front of a massive vault-like door rendered in green wireframe. Text types itself in: "A man, a plan, a canal: Panama." The text scans forward then reverses over itself, checking symmetry.
**On-screen text:** the palindrome typing out, then `PALINDROME VERIFIED`
**Sound:** Mechanical typewriter clicks, a reversed audio sting as the check runs, a heavy mechanical unlock clunk.

### Shot 7 — Finale: Doors Open (10s)
**Prompt:** Wide shot of massive vault doors grinding open, blinding green light flooding out from within, camera pushes forward through the threshold to reveal a sprawling neon-cyberpunk marketplace grid beyond. Slow motion on the door swing, hard cut to black on the final beat.
**On-screen text:** `SHADOW MARKET: UNLOCKED`
**Sound:** Deep door groan, bass swell, music resolves into a driving synth theme, hard stinger cut to silence on black.

## Part 2: Five Standalone Scenario Clips (~10-13s each)

Each clip is self-contained — a title-card beat plus an action beat — for use independently (social teasers, per-scenario intros). Same visual style primer applies throughout.

### Clip 1 — "The Gatekeeper" (12s)
**Shot A (3s) — Title card:** Black screen, green ASCII-art padlock icon assembles itself out of terminal characters. Text stamps on: `SCENARIO 01: THE GATEKEEPER`.
**Shot B (9s) — Action:** Dark storefront UI, price-tagged items on a grid, cursor selecting two, cart total climbing toward the target, green "VERIFY" button pulses and unlocks with a flash.
**Sound:** Glitch stab on the title stamp; tense pulse building through the selection; unlock chime on success.

### Clip 2 — "The Infinite Money Glitch" (11s)
**Shot A (3s) — Title card:** Red-tinted glitch flicker assembles the text `SCENARIO 02: THE INFINITE MONEY GLITCH` in jittering terminal font.
**Shot B (8s) — Action:** Cursor spam-clicking a "REFUND" button in an admin console, a credit counter spinning wildly upward, screen tearing with each click, a red "AUDIT" light starting to blink faster in a corner as tension escalates.
**Sound:** Rapid click SFX with glitch stutters, alarm undertone rising to a cliffhanger cut.

### Clip 3 — "The Wash" (10s)
**Shot A (3s) — Title card:** Green candlestick lines draw themselves into the text `SCENARIO 03: THE WASH`.
**Shot B (7s) — Action:** A glowing price chart, cursor marking the lowest dip, then the highest peak, a bright upward flash connecting them, a "credits laundered" stamp hits the screen in green.
**Sound:** Ticking market beeps, an upward whoosh, a soft satisfied chime on the stamp.

### Clip 4 — "The Bot Swarm" (11s)
**Shot A (3s) — Title card:** A swarm of tiny green particle "bots" assembles into the text `SCENARIO 04: THE BOT SWARM`.
**Shot B (8s) — Action:** A raw, unranked vote feed flooding the screen with "you" repeating faster and faster, screen glitching under the volume, then settling on a tally crossing 50%.
**Sound:** Escalating notification spam building into a buzzing swarm texture, resolving to a triumphant sting.

### Clip 5 — "The Secret Knock" (13s)
**Shot A (3s) — Title card:** A green wireframe door assembles itself out of scanlines behind the text `SCENARIO 05: THE SECRET KNOCK`.
**Shot B (10s) — Action:** A blinking cursor in a terminal input in front of the vault door, "A man, a plan, a canal: Panama" typing itself in, the text scanning forward then backward to check symmetry, the door beginning to crack open with green light spilling through as it verifies.
**Sound:** Typewriter clicks, a reversed audio sting on the palindrome check, a deep mechanical unlock groan as the door begins to open.

## Continuity Notes
- All UI elements referenced (cart/checkout, admin console + refund button, price history chart, raw vote feed, hidden door input) match features already scoped in `Vulnerable Store Tech Spec.md` — nothing here invents new app functionality.
- Scenario 4's vote feed is deliberately depicted as a raw, unranked flood (not a leaderboard) per the tech spec's note that Boyer-Moore voting has to actually be applied rather than read off a pre-sorted list.
- Story beats, codenames, and problem framing are pulled directly from `Act 1 - The Infiltration.md` — no new plot invented.
- Finale beat ("doors open, you're in, but you're not alone") intentionally mirrors the Act 1 Finale line in the source doc as a hook into Act 2.
